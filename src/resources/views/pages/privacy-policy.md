## What data do we collect?

Hub01 Shop may collect and process the following data:

* Account information, such as your username and email address
* Authentication information, such as your password hash
* Technical information, such as your IP address, browser information, request logs, and timestamps
* Project-related information that you provide, such as project names, descriptions, versions, files, links, tags, changelogs, and other project metadata
* User activity related to the service, such as favorites, collections, project ownership, downloads, and project statistics
* API-related information, such as API tokens, token names, token expiration dates, and API usage logs
* Analytics information, such as page views, referrers, browser type, operating system, device type, approximate location, and basic usage events
* Information you send to us when contacting support, submitting feedback, opening discussions, or making legal or privacy requests

Hub01 Shop does not intentionally collect special categories of personal data, such as health data, biometric data, political opinions, religious beliefs, or similar sensitive information.

The analytics data we collect may include:

* Page views
* Referrers
* Browser type
* Operating system
* Device type

Analytics data is processed on the basis of our legitimate interest in understanding and improving the service, maintaining reliability, and developing Hub01 Shop.

Analytics data is kept only for as long as necessary to understand general usage trends and improve the service. When it is no longer needed, it is deleted or aggregated.

## How long do we keep your data?

Hub01 Shop keeps personal data only for as long as necessary for the purposes described in this policy.

Current retention periods include:

* **IP addresses and server logs:** up to 30 days, unless longer retention is needed for security, abuse prevention, debugging, or legal reasons
* **Account information:** until you delete your account or request deletion
* **Project-related information:** for as long as the project remains available on the platform, unless deleted or anonymized
* **Favorites, collections, and account activity:** until you delete the relevant content, delete your account, or request deletion

* **Support, feedback, legal, and privacy requests:** for as long as necessary to respond to the request and keep reasonable records

When data is no longer needed, Hub01 Shop deletes or anonymizes it.

If you request account deletion, Hub01 Shop will delete or anonymize personal data associated with your account, unless retention is required for legal, security, or abuse-prevention reasons.

## Cookies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device by websites. They are commonly used to make websites work, keep users logged in, remember preferences, and support security features.

Hub01 Shop only uses essential cookies that are necessary for the website to function properly.

Hub01 Shop does not use analytics cookies or advertising cookies.

---

## How do we use cookies?

Hub01 Shop uses cookies to:

* Keep the website working correctly
* Manage user sessions
* Protect against Cross-Site Request Forgery attacks
* Remember cookie preferences
* Support account login and security

Hub01 Shop does not use cookies for advertising, marketing tracking, or analytics tracking.

---

## What types of cookies do we use?

Hub01 Shop uses essential cookies.

### Essential cookies

Essential cookies are necessary for the website to function properly and cannot be disabled through Hub01 Shop. They support core features such as security, login sessions, and user preferences.

The essential cookies we use include:

* **XSRF-TOKEN**
  Used to protect the website against Cross-Site Request Forgery attacks and help ensure secure interactions.

* **hub01-shop-session**
  Maintains your session while you navigate the website, including when you log in and use account features.

* **cookie_consent**
  Stores your cookie preference so the website can remember that you have seen or acknowledged the cookie notice.

These cookies are required for the proper operation of the website and are not used for marketing, advertising, or analytics.

If Hub01 Shop introduces analytics cookies, advertising cookies, or other non-essential cookies in the future, this policy will be updated and consent will be requested where required.

## How to manage cookies

You can set your browser to block or delete cookies. However, some website features may not function properly if essential cookies are blocked.

For example, blocking essential cookies may prevent you from logging in, staying logged in, or using account-related features.
